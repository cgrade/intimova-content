Why wetness varies (it’s not a flaw)
Natural lubrication changes with your cycle, stress, hydration, sleep, certain medications (including some contraceptives and antihistamines), and life stages like pregnancy, postpartum and menopause . Being dry doesn’t mean you’re not attracted to your partner or that something’s wrong — it’s ordinary variation. Treat it as information, not a verdict.

Stay hydrated and rested. General wellbeing supports natural lubrication (it’s helpful, not magic).
Mind medications. If a new medication lines up with new dryness, that’s worth noting and raising with a doctor.
