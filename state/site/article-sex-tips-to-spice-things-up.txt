Sex Tips & Techniques Sex Tips to Spice Things Up: Get a Little Freaky (Tastefully)

If sex has slipped into the same reliable routine, that’s normal — and completely fixable. These sex tips to spice things up are here to bring the heat back: a little freaky, a little playful, and classy enough that you’ll actually feel good trying them. Pick one to start; you don’t need all of them at once.
The golden rule: novelty plus communication . New sensations reignite desire, and talking about them makes it exciting instead of awkward.
1. Talk dirty (start small)
Words are wildly underused. A whispered "I’ve been thinking about you all day" builds heat before you touch. Start with what’s true and a little bold — you’ll find your voice fast.
2. Tease all day
Anticipation is the hottest foreplay. A suggestive text, a lingering touch as you pass, a promise for later — by the time you’re together, you’re both already there. (More in foreplay tips .)
3. Bring in a toy
The single easiest upgrade. A couples’ toy like the Rose Duo or a versatile Wand adds new sensation you can share — and it opens the door to trying more. New to it? How to talk to your partner about sex toys makes the conversation easy.
4. Blindfold and light restraint
Take away sight and every other sensation gets louder. A blindfold — or gentle, consensual restraint — adds an edge of anticipation and surrender without going to the deep end. (Our BDSM & kink edit is landing soon.)
5. Play with the senses
Warm breath, a cool ice cube, a feather, a silk scarf, contrasting temperatures — sensory play is freaky in the best way and costs nothing. Slow, surprising, delicious.
6. Change the script
Same bed, same time, same order — routine is the enemy of spark. Change the room, the time of day, or who’s in charge. Take turns where one of you is completely in control while the other simply receives.
7. Try gentle role play
You don’t need costumes. A scenario, a bit of pretend, a new dynamic for the night — role play lets you both step outside the everyday and be a little someone-else. Keep it playful.
Keep it consensual and fun
The only real rules: enthusiastic consent , a way to pause any time, and a sense of humour. The point isn’t to perform — it’s to explore together and enjoy it. Start with one new thing, see how it feels, and build from there.
The bottom line
Spicing things up isn’t about being someone you’re not — it’s novelty, anticipation and a willingness to play. Dirty talk, teasing, a toy, a blindfold, the senses, a change of script. Frequently asked
How can we spice things up in the bedroom? Break the routine: try dirty talk, tease and build anticipation all day, introduce a toy, use a blindfold, change the location or the time of day, and take turns being fully in charge. Novelty and communication are what reignite the spark — start with one new thing, not ten.
What are some freaky (but not intimidating) things to try? Blindfolds, light restraint, sensory play (ice, warmth, a feather), dirty talk, role play, and adding a couples’ toy. These bring an edge without going to the deep end — pick what excites you both and keep it consensual and fun.
How do we bring up trying something new? Raise it in a relaxed, low-pressure moment as something you’re curious about together — not a complaint. Frame it as ’I’d love to try…’ and invite their ideas too. Enthusiasm and consent make it exciting rather than awkward.
What’s the easiest way to add spice? A toy. Introducing a couples’ toy or a blindfold is the simplest, lowest-pressure way to make familiar sex feel new again — and it opens the door to trying more.
