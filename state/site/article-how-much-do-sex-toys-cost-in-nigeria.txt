What drives the price
Two toys can look similar and cost very differently. Here’s what you’re actually paying for:
Material. Body-safe, non-porous body-safe silicone costs more than cheap plastic or "jelly" — and it’s the single most important thing (see how to choose a body-safe sex toy ).
Motor and features. Rechargeable batteries, multiple patterns, air-pulse technology and waterproofing all add cost — and genuine value.
Build quality. A toy that lasts years is cheaper per use than one you replace in months.
Why the cheapest is rarely the best value
You’ll see toys advertised very cheaply, especially informally. Be careful: the lowest prices usually mean porous, unlabelled materials that can’t be fully cleaned — a real hygiene risk, and a false economy when they wear out fast. Spending a little more on a body-safe silicone toy isn’t a splurge; it’s the sensible choice. Quality here is about safety, not luxury.
