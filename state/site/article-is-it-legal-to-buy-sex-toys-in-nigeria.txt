The short answer

What Nigerian law actually addresses
Nigerian law, like most public-decency frameworks, concerns itself with things like public obscenity and the sale of explicit material to minors — not with a discerning adult privately choosing a wellness product for themselves. The distinction that matters is public versus private : public indecency is one thing; a sealed, discreet parcel delivered to your home for your own personal use is another entirely.
So the anxiety many people carry — that they’re doing something forbidden — usually doesn’t match what the law is actually aimed at.
