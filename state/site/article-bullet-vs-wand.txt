If you’re weighing a bullet vs wand vibrator for your first toy, here’s the reassuring part: both are beloved for good reason, and neither is "better" — they simply feel different. This guide gives you the short version, so you can choose with confidence.
The quick answer: a bullet is precise, discreet and low-commitment; a wand is broad, powerful and forgiving. Which suits you depends on whether you want subtlety or strength .
The Bullet — precise, discreet, easy
Small, quiet and pinpoint. A rechargeable bullet gives focused stimulation exactly where you want it, slips into any bag, and is the most recommended first toy for a reason: it’s unintimidating and completely discreet.
Best if you like: control, subtlety, and something you can travel with or tuck away. It’s also the most affordable entry point.
The Wand — broad, powerful, forgiving
Bigger, with a cushioned head and deep, rumbly power that many people first buy simply as a body massager. The Wand is broad rather than precise, and famously easy to use — you don’t aim it so much as rest it.
Best if you like: strong, external sensation over a wider area. A layer of clothing can soften it at first if the power feels like a lot.
Bullet vs wand: the quick guide
Want quiet and discreet? → Bullet.
Want power and ease? → Wand.
On a tighter budget? → Bullet.
Also want a real body massager? → Wand.
Genuinely not sure? → The bullet is the gentler, lower-commitment place to start.

Frequently asked
Is a bullet or a wand better for beginners? The bullet is the gentler, lower-commitment place to start — small, quiet and discreet. The wand is powerful and broad, which some beginners love and others find intense at first (a layer of clothing softens it).
What’s the difference between a bullet and a wand vibrator? A bullet gives precise, pinpoint vibration and is small and discreet. A wand gives broad, deep, powerful vibration over a larger area. Bullet is about control and subtlety; wand is about strong, easy, external sensation.
Are wands only for intimate use? Not at all — many people first buy a wand as a genuine body massager for tense shoulders and backs. The deep, rumbly power is the same reason it’s a classic either way.
Rechargeable or battery? Rechargeable (USB) is more convenient and consistent; battery is the most affordable entry point. Both our bullets are body-safe silicone — it comes down to your preference.
